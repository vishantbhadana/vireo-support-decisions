# Priya: fix routing before allocating the two hires

**29 September 2026 · Recommendation: a four-week assisted-routing pilot**

Please hold the allocation of the two hires while we test the route into Billing. The export does not measure active handling effort or staffed capacity, so it cannot establish which team needs another employee.

In April–June, Billing received **450 tickets (19.0%)** and Logistics **373 (15.8%)**. Chat Frontline, at **648**, was larger than either. Those are first-assigned queues, not necessarily the teams doing the work. Logistics took a median **25.93 elapsed hours** to resolve its intake, versus **1.91 hours** for Billing. That supports investigating Logistics, but waiting for a courier is not the same as an agent working for a day. Specialist warranty work must be assessed separately.

**One measurable target.** We found **101 Billing tickets** with clear delivery evidence and **134 recorded transfers**. Test reducing transfers in comparable tickets from **1.33 to 0.66 per ticket**. At the policy cost of **Rs 305 per transfer**, halving 134 transfers is worth **Rs 20,435 a quarter** in released capacity at unchanged volume. This is a target—not a realised cash saving—and is much smaller than the proposed **Rs 9 lakh/year** hiring spend. We cannot yet say either hire is unnecessary.

**What happens next.** Neha runs four weeks of human-approved delivery routing; compare with the existing route and track transfers, first-response breaches and serious misroutes. Expand only if transfers halve without worsening response service. Sameer supplies staffed hours, active handling effort and backlog age before we revisit hiring. Arjun verifies the cost assumptions. Low-confidence suggestions remain with an agent: our small 60-ticket AI-assisted audit matched 56 categories; all four errors went to review. Independent staff validation is still required.

**Two separate Finance checks.** Fifteen Q2 orders show refund and replacement across separate tickets, and seven “Goodwill/Other” refunds exceed Rs 500. Verify fulfilment, reason codes and approvals before taking action. These are review flags, not proven loss; they are not included in the routing estimate.

**Trust the boundaries.** The tool separates intake from resolver charts, corrects legacy resolution timezones and leaves missing transfer history unknown. The export averages about 182 tickets/week in Q2, versus the stated 650; reconcile coverage before scaling any result. Monthly charts are useful evidence, but volume alone should not decide the hires.
